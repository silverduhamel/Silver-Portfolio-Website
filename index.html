Hello! My name is Silver Duhamel

I'm a hobbyist/maker who has many projects spanning many fields.

My most notable projects thusfar are my Deployable Inline Skates, and my home lab, named ANDROMEDA

About my skate project:

The genesis of this project came from my irritation with needing to switch to an entirely separate shoe for rollerblading. The process of switching over took a long time, and having to carry the other pair of shoes was a pain.

You might be questioning: “But Silver, what about Heelys??” To which I raise the same point. Heelys have this awkward waddle that comes from keeping the wheels installed when not rolling. They also make slipping and falling much easier. The safety hazard of Heelys was the other contributor to this project.

My current design is not yet deployable, as I first plan to test the viability of the structure I've been working on.


About ANDROMEDA

ANDROMEDA is the name of my entire home lab. It hosts many things, including this very website that you're reading this on!

It is currently made up of primarily Raspberry Pi SBCs (Single Board Computers), but also includes a decommissioned daily laptop. This laptop is used for its high-end graphics card, for failure detection on my three 3d printers. It is named MESSIER-31, after the black hole at the center of the Andromeda galaxy.

My three machines are:

  • Sirius, a FlashForge Creator Pro
  • Erid, a first-gen Creality Ender 3
  • Nebula, an Ender 3 V2.

All three machines have Klipper installed, and two of the three have been converted to BIGTREETECH motherboards, for their 32-bit STM processors.

Erid is currently the primary workhorse of the three. Nebula usually makes small and/or intricate models at low speed, and Sirius is the (WIP) high-speed prototyping machine. Sirius itself is currently in prototyping, as I'm working to push it to its limits.


My main computing cluster is Orion, and it houses several Pi boards.

  • Raspberry Pi 5: Hosts my VPN connection from anywhere in the world to my house.
  • Raspberry Pi 4: Hosts my cloudflared install, which keeps my home network secure while hosting the websites.
  • Raspberry Pi Zero W: What normally serves websites via NGinx, but is currently out of service due to repeated DDoS attacks on my websites.
  • Raspberry Pi Zero W 2: Hosts an automated bot on Discord, used as an accessibility tool.

I've yet to name the individual boards, but that may come soon.

This website is actively under construction, which is why it's so bare-bones. It will be built further in the future.
